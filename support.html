How to play

A slab slides back and forth over the tower. Tap the screen (on Mac: click, or press Space or Return) to drop it. The part that overlaps the slab below stays; the rest falls off. If you miss completely, the run ends.

Questions

What counts as a perfect drop?
Landing the slab almost exactly on the one below. Perfect drops keep the full width, play a rising note, and from the third in a row the slab grows back a little.
How do I unlock themes?
Ocean at a score of 20, Forest at 40, Aurora at 75, Mono after 25 games, and Neon after 10 perfect drops in a row. Pick them under Themes on the home screen.
I hear no sound.
On iPhone and iPad, sound follows the silent switch and your volume. Also check Settings › Sound inside the game.
Is my progress synced between devices?
Not yet. Progress is saved only on the device you play on.
How do I reset my best score?
Open Settings in the game and choose Reset progress. This clears your best score, stats and theme unlocks on that device.
