HUELOFT

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Applies to Hueloft on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Hueloft does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To work, the game saves a few things locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage:

  • your best score and play statistics (games played, slabs stacked, perfect drops, longest streak)
  • which color themes you have unlocked and selected
  • your sound and haptics settings

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time in Settings › Reset progress inside the game, or by deleting the app.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a game, Hueloft creates an image of your tower and opens Apple's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it, and what happens next is governed by the app or service you pick.

Third parties

Hueloft contains no third-party code, SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services. Apple may collect crash reports and usage data if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your device settings; that data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Hueloft is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
